How to read a location · the cun

Measuring with the body's own ruler

Traditional locations are given in cun (pronounced "tsoon") — body-inches measured on the person being treated, not a fixed length. The width of their thumb at the knuckle is 1 cun; the four fingers held together at the second knuckle is 3 cun. So a point "3 cun above the inner ankle" sits four finger-widths up, whatever the body size.

Press with a fingertip or thumb on a tender, slightly hollow spot, building to firm, steady pressure that feels strong but not sharp. Hold or make small circles for around one to two minutes, breathing slowly. Stop if it ever feels sharp, electric or genuinely painful.

Please read · safety

A few sensible cautions

This is a reference for traditional wellness practice and general interest — it is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose or treat disease. See a qualified practitioner or doctor for anything persistent or serious.

Several points are traditionally avoided during pregnancy (notably LI 4, SP 6, BL 60, BL 67 and GB 21) as they strongly move energy in the body. Points near the eyes, throat, arteries and the perineum are best left to a trained practitioner; press these only very gently, if at all. Never apply pressure over broken skin, varicose veins, recent injuries or wounds.

Using the oils · safety

Working with essential oils safely

Essential oils are highly concentrated. Dilute before any skin contact — generally a few drops per tablespoon of carrier oil (around 1–3%) — and never swallow them. Patch-test first, keep them away from eyes, and store them out of reach of children and pets.

Some need extra care: citrus oils (bergamot, lemon, lime, grapefruit) can be phototoxic, so keep treated skin out of strong sun. Cinnamon, clove, oregano and similar "hot" oils are strong irritants and need heavy dilution. Wintergreen needs particular caution. If pregnant, breastfeeding, epileptic, or on medication, check with a qualified aromatherapist or doctor first. The chakra associations are offered in the energetic tradition of aromatherapy, not as clinical claims.

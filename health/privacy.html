Privacy Policy

Neeve Health · Effective 12 June 2026

Neeve helps you understand your body with kindness. We take the privacy of your health information seriously, and we built Neeve to hold it carefully. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Who we are

Neeve is operated from Sydney, Australia. You can reach us any time at hello@noti.com.au.

What we collect

  • Account details — your mobile number (used to sign you in) and a display name, if you give one.
  • Health and wellbeing data — readings from a paired Neeve ring (such as heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, respiration, sleep and skin temperature); the things you log in check-ins (symptoms, mood, energy, cycle and similar); and any notes you write.
  • Apple Health data — if you choose to connect Apple Health, we read the data types you approve (initially your workouts and steps) to show your movement alongside how your body is doing. We only ever read from Apple Health; we never write to it.
  • App usage and diagnostics — basic events about how the app is working (for example, the app opening, or a sync succeeding or failing), so we can keep it reliable.

Apple Health (HealthKit). Data we read from Apple Health is used only to provide features inside Neeve. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, we do not share it with data brokers, and we do not use it for any form of cross-app tracking. You can disconnect Apple Health at any time in Settings, or revoke access in the Apple Health app.

How we use it

  • To show you your own data and the patterns within it.
  • To power Alys, the quiet observational presence in the app, which may process your data (including via a third-party AI provider) to describe what it notices in plain language.
  • To produce reports you can choose to share with your own doctor.
  • To keep the app working and improve its reliability.

We never use your health data to advertise to you, and we never sell it.

Who it's shared with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We use a small number of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract, only to run Neeve:

  • Cloudflare — app hosting and delivery.
  • Supabase — secure database storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — language processing for Alys (not used to train their models).
  • Twilio — sending your sign-in code by SMS.

We may disclose information if required by law. Your data may be stored on servers outside Australia; where it is, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — you can view your data in the app and ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion — you can delete your data and close your account from within the app, or by emailing us. Deleting removes your health data from our systems.
  • Withdraw consent — you can disconnect Apple Health, turn off sections, or stop using the app at any time.

Keeping it safe

Your data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted, and we keep your information only for as long as you have an account or as required by law. If we ever became aware of a data breach likely to cause you serious harm, we would notify you and the OAIC as required.

Not a medical device

Neeve describes patterns to support your own understanding and your conversations with a health professional. It does not diagnose, treat, or predict any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for medical advice. Always speak with your doctor about anything that concerns you.

Children

Neeve is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

If we update this policy, we'll change the date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app. Questions, requests, or complaints: hello@noti.com.au. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

8. Taking card payments

  • Stripe will ask for identity and business details. That is a legal requirement on Stripe, and card payments cannot be switched on without it.
  • Money can be held by Stripe — for unusual amounts, disputes or verification. We will tell you what we know about why, and what is needed.
  • Disputes and chargebacks are yours. If a customer disputes a payment and wins, the amount and any dispute fee come out of your Stripe balance.
  • Noti does not see or store your customers' card numbers.

Noti records consent, honours opt-outs, and refuses to send where consent has been withdrawn — but having consent in the first place is your responsibility.
